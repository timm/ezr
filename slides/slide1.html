1. Counting vs boosting: 4 points, 6x the speed

50 MOOT data sets × 20 shuffles. Both learners see the same first 75% of the same shuffle and are scored on the same last 25%. ∗ = statistically the same as the best, by cliff’s delta and Kolmogorov–Smirnov.

Blue = min.py ahead, orange = LightGBM. Every outright win for counting is a small file; the two worst losses are letter at 20000 rows and tic-tac-toe. That is the generative/discriminative crossover: counting wins while data is scarce, boosting wins once it is not. Seconds are the learning only — interpreter start-up is charged to neither.
